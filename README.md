# Russia · Syria Strategic Axis (September 2026)
### Tactical 3D Command Center & Bilateral Security Memorandum Analytics

Interactive 3D Geospatial Command Platform visualizing sovereign geopolitical boundaries, 11 strategic bilateral corridors, accredited diplomatic rosters, energy & grain lifelines, and the historic August 9, 2026 Memorandum.

---

## ⚡ Key Capabilities
- **Tactical 3D Globe & Mercator Projections**: Full hardware-accelerated WebGL cartography via MapLibre GL with zero lag.
- **Sovereign 2026 Boundaries**: Complete, accurate borders for the Russian Federation and Syrian Arab Republic.
- **11 Strategic Corridors**: Real-time rendering of transcontinental air bridges (Moscow–Khmeimim), maritime energy routes (Novorossiysk–Baniyas), grain express lines (Novorossiysk–Latakia), naval logistics lines ("Syrian Express"), coastal pipelines, and the African strategic axis.
- **Interactive Chronology Scrubber (1944–2026)**: Historical time-machine exploring 1944 diplomatic recognition, 1980 Treaty of Friendship, 2015 Khmeimim accord, and the 2026 Bilateral Memorandum.
- **Accredited Diplomatic & Military Rosters**: Embassy personnel and military training unit complement breakdowns.
- **Zero-Glitch Responsive Design**: Optimized across mobile portrait, mobile landscape, tablets, and 4K desktop command displays.
